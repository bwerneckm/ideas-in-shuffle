Two years into studying stablecoins, I can finally articulate the problem I faced at the start: too much noise, not enough signal. The space is cluttered with promotional threads, surface-level takes, and thought pieces that gesture at importance without explaining machinery. What I needed—and what you’re holding now—was a curated path through the essential work written by people who actually understand payment infrastructure, monetary systems, and the cryptographic rails underneath.

This reading list represents what amounts to a university course. I’ve spent two years following Nic Carter’s monetary analysis and infrastructure deep dives (including his Murmurations blog), Simon Taylor’s fintech lens, and the a16z crypto team’s systematic breakdowns. These writers don’t hype; they explain. They show you the rulebook, the incentives, and the failure modes. That’s what makes this collection worth your time.

## How to use this list

- **If you’re starting from scratch**: Begin with Section 1 (Foundations & Primers) and move sequentially. The primers establish definitions and mental models you’ll need later.
- **If you’re topic-focused**: Jump directly to the section that matters—cross-border payments, regulatory frameworks, technical architecture.
- **If you’re already conversant**: Skip to Section 5 (Critical Analysis) for the sharpest debates and forward-looking questions.

## 1. Foundations & Primers

**Core understanding of what stablecoins are and why they matter**

Start here. These pieces establish the taxonomy, mechanisms, and historical context.

- **[Cryptodollars](https://niccarter.info/wp-content/uploads/Cryptodollars-Castle-Island-Ventures-2020-07-07.pdf)** – [Nic Carter](https://x.com/nic__carter?lang=en) (Castle Island Ventures, 2020)The foundational paper. Carter introduces “cryptodollars” as a concept and lays out the monetary and technical vision. Must-read.
- **[Stablecoins in 1,000 words](https://notes.mtb.xyz/p/stablecoins-1000-words)** – [Matt Brown](https://www.linkedin.com/in/matttb/)Perfect entry point. Clear, compact explanation of stablecoin types and basic mechanics.
- **[How to Think About Stablecoins](https://fintechtakes.com/articles/2025-01-10/how-to-think-about-stablecoins/)** – Alex JohnsonProvides a mental framework for analysing value and use cases without getting lost in technical weeds.
- **[Understanding Stablecoins Banking History](https://a16zcrypto.com/posts/article/understanding-stablecoins-banking-history/)** – a16z cryptoEssential context. Shows how stablecoins relate to historical banking evolution—private money issuance, reserve backing, and trust infrastructure.
- **[Stablecoin Guide: What, Why, How](https://a16zcrypto.com/posts/article/stablecoin-guide-what-why-how/)** – a16z cryptoComprehensive fundamentals guide covering the basics with the a16z team’s characteristic precision.
- **[A Stablecoin Primer](https://jasshah.substack.com/p/a-stablecoin-primer)** – Jas ShahAnother strong introduction to concepts, mechanisms, and ecosystem dynamics.

## 2. Infrastructure & Technical Architecture

**How stablecoins work technically and their position in the payments stack**

Once you understand what stablecoins are, you need to understand the machinery: blockchain rails, payment flows, trust infrastructure, and interoperability.

- **[Situating Stablecoins in the Payments Landscape](https://murmurationstwo.substack.com/p/situating-stablecoins-in-the-payments)** – [Nic Carter](https://x.com/nic__carter?lang=en)Carter’s infrastructure analysis is some of the best in the space. This piece maps where stablecoins sit among traditional payment methods—schemes, rails, and settlement layers.
- **[Yes We Really Do Want to Trust Crypto](https://murmurationstwo.substack.com/p/yes-we-really-do-want-to-trust-crypto)** – [Nic Carter](https://x.com/nic__carter?lang=en)The trust infrastructure underpinning stablecoin systems. Carter’s writing consistently cuts through the “trustless” rhetoric to show where institutional and technical trust actually matters.
- **[BVNK Stablecoin Infrastructure](https://jasshah.substack.com/p/bvnk-stablecoin-infrastructure)** – Jas ShahExamines modern stablecoin infrastructure providers and their role in the ecosystem.
- **[Blockchains, Banks, Asset Managers, Fintechs](https://a16zcrypto.com/posts/article/blockchains-banks-asset-managers-fintechs/)** – a16z cryptoHow traditional financial institutions are integrating with blockchain infrastructure. Shows the convergence happening beneath the surface.
- **[On-Chain Foreign Exchange and Cross-Border Payments](https://www.circle.com/blog/on-chain-foreign-exchange-and-cross-border-payments)** – CircleTechnical deep dive on implementing FX and payments on-chain. Gets into settlement mechanics and liquidity.
- **[Squaring the Circle](https://dirtroads.substack.com/p/52-squaring-the-circle)** – [Luca Prosperi](https://x.com/LucaProsperi)Technical analysis of stablecoin mechanics and market dynamics from another sharp voice in the space.
- **[The 10 Minute Guide to Tether](https://dirtroads.substack.com/p/55-the-10-minute-guide-to-tether)** – [Luca Prosperi](https://x.com/LucaProsperi)Comprehensive overview of USDT’s technical architecture and business model. Love it or hate it, Tether dominates—understand why.
- **[The Crypto Banking System](https://cryptobanking.network/the-crypto-banking-system/)**Deep dive into how stablecoins function within the broader crypto financial system.

## 3. Real-World Applications & Market Dynamics

**Use cases, adoption patterns, and market structure**

This is where stablecoins leave the whiteboard and enter the real economy. Cross-border flows, emerging market dollarisation, corporate adoption, and market evolution.

### Cross-Border & Emerging Markets

- **[Cross-Border Payments in ~1,000 words](https://notes.mtb.xyz/p/cross-border-payments-1000-words)** – [Matt Brown](https://www.linkedin.com/in/matttb/)Overview of how cross-border payments work today and where stablecoins fit into that machinery.
- **[It’s Time Local Went Global](https://activantcapital.com/research/real-time-payments)** – Activant CapitalThe case for stablecoins in international commerce. Well-argued and grounded in payment system realities.
- **[Getting to the Crux of Stablecoin Utility](https://medium.com/letters-from-the-savannah/getting-to-the-crux-of-stablecoin-utility-7e53fea29286)** – [Josh from Parafi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joshua-solesbury-206589170/)Analysis of how stablecoins power the DeFi ecosystem—collateral, liquidity, composability.
- **[Stablecoins: The Emerging Market Story](https://castleisland.vc/writing/stablecoins-the-emerging-market-story/)** – [Nic Carter](https://x.com/nic__carter?lang=en) (at Castle Island)Deep analysis of adoption in developing economies.
- **[Making Sense of Tether on Tron](https://liamhorne.com/stablecoins)** – Liam HorneWhy USDT on Tron became the dominant stablecoin for global payments, and how Ethereum can compete. Excellent on-chain data analysis.
- **[Dollarisation 2.0: How Stablecoins Unlock Emerging Markets – VIDEO](https://read.readwise.io/search/read/01hr3j9fq53f8gzhdjvfgw82qe)** – [Nic Carter](http://Nic Carter’s Old Articles) & Martin CarricaDeep exploration of stablecoin adoption in countries with unstable currencies. Carter’s monetary analysis shines here.

### Industry Adoption & Market Analysis

- **[State of Crypto Report 2024: New Data on Stablecoins](https://a16zcrypto.com/posts/article/state-of-crypto-report-2024/)** – a16z cryptoComprehensive market data showing stablecoin growth hitting all-time highs. The a16z team brings real numbers and trend analysis.
- **[Stripe Acquires Bridge: Stablecoins, Platform of Platforms](https://stratechery.com/2024/stripe-acquires-bridge-stablecoins-platform-of-platforms/)** – Ben ThompsonDeep analysis of Stripe’s $1.1B acquisition signalling institutional adoption. Thompson’s strategic lens is always worth reading.
- **[Why Stripe Spent $1.1B on Bridge](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/wy-stripe-spent-11bn-stablecoin-startup-bridge-simon-taylor--vr6ve/)** – [Simon Taylor](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sytaylor/?originalSubdomain=uk)Simon’s financial infrastructure perspective on Stripe’s strategic stablecoin play. He’s one of the sharpest observers of fintech-crypto convergence.
- **[Stablecoins 2030](https://www.citigroup.com/rcs/citigpa/storage/public/GPS_Report_Stablecoins_2030.pdf)** – Citi GPS ReportInstitutional research on the future trajectory of stablecoin markets. Big bank perspective with actual numbers.
- **[Crypto Neobanks](https://www.bankless.com/read/crypto-neobanks)** – BanklessHow crypto-native banking services are being built on stablecoin rails.
- **[PayPal PYUSD: Does It Really Matter?](https://dirtroads.substack.com/p/57-paypal-pyusd-does-it-really-matter)** – [Luca Prosperi](https://x.com/LucaProsperi)Analysis of corporate stablecoin initiatives and their market impact. Healthy scepticism applied.

### Market Structure & Evolution

- **[The Great Onchain Migration](https://panteracapital.com/blockchain-letter/the-great-onchain-migration/)** – Pantera CapitalVision for how stablecoins enable the shift to onchain finance. Forward-looking but grounded.
- **[The Stablecoin Duopoly is Ending](https://murmurationstwo.substack.com/p/the-stablecoin-duopoly-is-ending)** – [Nic Carter](https://x.com/nic__carter?lang=en)Analysis of changing market dynamics and new entrants. Carter tracking competitive shifts as they happen.
- **[Stablecoins Are Better](https://www.fintechbrainfood.com/p/stablecoins-are-better)** – [Simon Taylor](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sytaylor/?originalSubdomain=uk)Why stablecoins represent a superior payment infrastructure. Simon making the case clearly.
- **[Visa Crypto Report](https://www.visa.co.uk/content/dam/VCOM/regional/ve/unitedkingdom/PDF/vca/uk-vca-crypto.pdf)** – Visa UKPayment network perspective on stablecoin integration. Useful to see how the incumbents are thinking.

## 4. Regulatory & Policy Landscape

**Global regulatory frameworks and policy perspectives**

Stablecoins operate at the intersection of technology, finance, and regulation. Understanding the policy landscape is non-optional.

- **[Statement on Stablecoins](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/statement-stablecoins-040425)** – SEC Division of Corporation FinanceSEC’s official view on certain stablecoins not being securities. Critical for U.S. regulatory clarity.
- **[The CLARITY Act – Why It Matters, What to Know, and What to Do](https://a16zcrypto.com/posts/article/genius-act-clarity-act-crypto-legislation-explained/)** – a16z cryptoBreaking down proposed U.S. stablecoin legislation. The a16z policy team does excellent work translating regulatory proposals.
- **[FCA – Regulating cryptoassets Phase 1: Stablecoins](https://www.fca.org.uk/publication/discussion/dp23-4.pdf)** – UK Financial Conduct AuthorityUK’s approach to stablecoin regulation. Dense but important for understanding the British framework.
- **[The EU’s MiCA Framework: The One Regulation to Rule Them All](https://paddihansen.substack.com/p/the-eus-mica-framework)** – Patrick HansenComprehensive breakdown of Europe’s Markets in Crypto-Assets regulation—its genesis, goals, rules, and global implications.
- **[American Leadership in the Digital Financial Technology](https://readwise.io/reader/document_raw_content/345006412)** – White HousePolicy perspective on U.S. strategic advantages in stablecoins. Shows how policymakers are thinking about competition and innovation.

Carter bringing banking history to bear on crypto debates.

- **[Will the real stablecoin please stand up](https://www.bis.org/publ/bppdf/bispap141.pdf)** – BISComparison of different stablecoin models and their trade-offs. Central bank perspective with technical depth.
